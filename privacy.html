Privacy policy

Salvo Command, by playbyear. Last updated November 25, 2026.

Salvo Command is a naval strategy game you play against the computer or against a friend online. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it never asks for your name or any other personal information. This page explains the little information the game handles and where it goes.

What stays on your device

A random code the game makes up for your device, and the code of the last online game you played so you can rejoin it, are kept on your phone or computer: in the browser's storage on the website, and in the app's own storage in the iPhone app. A game against the computer is played entirely on your device and nothing about it is sent anywhere. Deleting the app, or clearing the website's data in your browser, deletes what's stored.

Online games, if you play them

Playing a friend online is optional. When you host or join an online game, the game is stored in an online database run with Google's Firebase service, so both players' devices can follow it. An online game holds:

  • its four-letter game code,
  • the weapons and turn rules chosen for it,
  • where each player placed their fleet, and every shot and weapon used,
  • the time of the last move, and
  • each player's random device code, so the game knows which side you are when you come back to it.

Nothing in an online game identifies you. There are no names, messages or chat. Nobody can list or browse the games; a game can only be found with its code, which you share with your friend yourself. A game isn't removed the moment it ends. Once nobody has touched it for a week it can be cleared away, which happens when its code comes up again for a new game.

Google's part

While you're in an online game, Google receives your device's internet address as part of the connection, as any website does. Google handles it under its own terms as a service provider; the game doesn't keep or use it. You can read Firebase's privacy information.

Children

The game isn't aimed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect personal information from them. It never asks for any.

Changes

If the game ever handles information differently, this page will be updated first, with a new date at the top.

Contact

Questions, problems, or anything about your privacy: use the contact form, and say that it's about Salvo Command. You don't need an account, and only the game's developer sees what you send. Add your email address only if you'd like a reply; it's used just to answer you. Messages are kept in Google Forms, under Google's terms.
