Salvo Command, by playbyear.

Salvo Command is a naval strategy game for one or two players on a 10 by 14 grid. Hide your fleet, hunt the enemy's, and sink all five of their ships first. Optional advanced weapons add attack squadrons, missiles, torpedoes and sonar. It works fully with VoiceOver and other screen readers.

Get help or send feedback

Use the contact form for bug reports, questions and ideas, and say that it's about Salvo Command. You don't need an account. Add your email address if you'd like a reply. For a bug, it helps to say what you were doing and whether you use VoiceOver.

Common questions

How do I play?

The full rules are in the game, under How to play. In short: place your five ships, start the battle, and take turns firing at the enemy grid. A ship sinks when every square of it has been hit.

How do I play on a touch screen?

Type a square such as C7 in the Place at box during setup, or in the Fire at box during battle, and press the button next to it. You can also double-tap squares on the grids. The Weapon menu picks an advanced weapon, and the Repeat last message and Fleet status buttons read the latest news.

What are advanced weapons and Target plus 1?

Both are optional rules chosen before a game. Advanced weapons gives each side attack squadrons, an antiaircraft battery, a Tomahawk missile, harpoon missiles, torpedoes and sonar. With Target plus 1, a hit earns another turn. How to play in the game explains each one.

Does it need the internet?

No. Games against the computer work offline. Only playing a friend online needs a connection.

How do I play a friend?

Choose the weapons and turn rules, then press Host an online game. You get a four-letter code and an invite link to send to your friend. They type the code under Online play, or open the link. A friend on the website and a friend in the app can play each other.

I closed the game in the middle of an online match.

Open the game again and press Rejoin game, or open the invite link again. The match picks up where it left off.

Does the computer cheat?

No. It only knows what you would know: the results of its own shots and searches, and which of your ships are sunk.

Does it have ads, purchases or tracking?

No ads, nothing to buy, no accounts and no tracking. See the privacy policy.
