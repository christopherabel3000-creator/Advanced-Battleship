## Description (4,000)

Salvo Command is a naval strategy game of hidden fleets and well-aimed shots. Place five ships on a 10 by 14 grid, then take turns firing at the enemy's waters. Sink all five of their ships before they sink yours.

FIVE SHIPS, ONE SEA
Command an aircraft carrier, a battleship, a light missile cruiser, a fast attack sub and a special operations craft. Place each one yourself, or let the game scatter them for you.

ADVANCED WEAPONS, IF YOU WANT THEM
Turn on advanced weapons and both fleets get more than guns. Launch attack squadrons to search the sea in an X or plus pattern, and strike the first ship they find. Shoot enemy squadrons down with your antiaircraft battery. Fire a Tomahawk at a whole block of sea, send harpoons down a line, run a torpedo across a row, or ping with sonar. Lose a ship and you lose its weapons.

TARGET PLUS 1
An optional rule for faster, fiercer games: every hit earns another turn.

A COMPUTER THAT PLAYS FAIR
The computer opponent only knows what you would know: the results of its own shots and searches, and which of your ships have sunk. It never peeks at your fleet.

PLAY A FRIEND ONLINE
Host a game and share a four-letter code or an invite link. Each of you places your fleet on your own device. Leave and come back whenever you like: the game is waiting.

MADE FOR VOICEOVER
Salvo Command was designed from the start to be fully playable with VoiceOver. Every square reads what's on it, every shot is announced, and a battle log keeps the whole game in order. Type a square to place a ship or fire, check your fleet's status at any time, and repeat the last message with one tap. With a keyboard, arrow keys move around the grids.

SIMPLE AND PRIVATE
Games against the computer work offline. No ads. Nothing to buy. No accounts. No tracking.
