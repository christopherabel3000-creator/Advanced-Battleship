## Description (4,000)

Salvo Command is a naval strategy game of hidden fleets and well-aimed shots. Place five ships on a 10 by 14 grid, then take turns firing at the enemy's waters. Sink all five of their ships before they sink yours.

FIVE SHIPS, ONE SEA
Command an aircraft carrier, a battleship, a light missile cruiser, a fast attack sub and a special operations craft. Place each one yourself, or let the game scatter them for you.

ADVANCED WEAPONS, IF YOU WANT THEM
Turn on advanced weapons and both fleets get more than guns. Launch attack squadrons to search the sea in an X or plus pattern, and strike the first ship they find. Shoot enemy squadrons down with your antiaircraft battery. Fire a Tomahawk at a whole block of sea, send harpoons down a line, run a torpedo across a row, or ping with sonar. Lose a ship and you lose its weapons.

TARGET PLUS 1
An optional rule for faster, fiercer games: every hit earns another turn.

A COMPUTER THAT PLAYS FAIR
The computer opponent only knows what you would know: the results of its own shots and searches, and which of your ships have sunk. It never peeks at your fleet.

PLAY A FRIEND ONLINE
Host a game and share a four-letter code or an invite link. Each of you places your fleet on your own device. Leave and come back whenever you like: the game is waiting.

MADE FOR VOICEOVER
Salvo Command was designed from the start to be fully playable with VoiceOver. Every square reads what's on it, every shot is announced, and a battle log keeps the whole game in order. Type a square to place a ship or fire, check your fleet's status at any time, and repeat the last message with one tap. With a keyboard, arrow keys move around the grids.

SIMPLE AND PRIVATE
Games against the computer work offline. No ads. Nothing to buy. No accounts. No tracking.

## Notes for the reviewer

Apple asks every new app for six things in this field: a screen recording made on a real iPhone, and answers 2 to 6 below. Only the recording link changes.

Salvo Command is a complete naval strategy game for one or two players, built to be fully playable with VoiceOver by a developer who plays with a screen reader. The build was tested on a physical iPhone through TestFlight before submission.

1. SCREEN RECORDING
Recorded on a physical iPhone running the latest iOS, starting from launching the app: RECORDING_LINK
The voice heard in the recording is VoiceOver, since the developer plays the game with a screen reader.
The app has no account registration or login, no user-generated content (players share only game moves; there are no names, messages or chat), and no paid content or purchases. So those flows don't exist to show.

2. PURPOSE AND AUDIENCE
A naval strategy game on a 10 by 14 grid. Each player hides five ships and takes turns firing at the other's waters; the first to sink the whole enemy fleet wins. Optional advanced weapons add search aircraft, missiles, torpedoes and sonar, and an optional rule gives another turn after every hit. It can be played against a computer opponent or against a friend online. It is for strategy game fans, and especially for blind and low-vision players: it was designed for VoiceOver from the start, so every square and shot is read out, and ships can be placed and weapons fired by typing.

3. HOW TO USE IT
No account, login or sample files are needed. The game opens ready to play against the computer. Tap Random placement, then Start battle. Type a square such as C7 in the Fire at box and tap Fire, or double-tap a square on the Enemy waters grid. To try the advanced weapons, choose Advanced weapons before starting, then pick a weapon from the Weapon menu. The rules are under How to play at the bottom.
To try online play with one device: tap Host an online game, which shows a four-letter code and an invite link. Open the link in a browser on a second device or a computer to join as the other player (the website and the app can play each other). Both players place their fleets and tap I'm ready, and the host fires first.

4. EXTERNAL SERVICES
Only one, and it is optional: Google Firebase Realtime Database, used for online games between two friends who share a four-letter code. There are no authentication services, payment processors, ad networks, analytics or AI services. The whole game is bundled in the app, and games against the computer run offline.

5. REGIONS
The app works the same in every region. It is in English only. There is no region-specific content or behavior.

6. REGULATED INDUSTRIES AND THIRD-PARTY MATERIAL
Not applicable. The app is not in a regulated industry. It contains no third-party or licensed material: the name, artwork, text and code are original.
