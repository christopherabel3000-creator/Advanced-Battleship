      How to play

      The grid has rows A to J from top to bottom and columns 1 to 14 from left to right. A1 is the top left corner and J14 is the bottom right.

      • Arrow keys move around a grid. Home and End go to the start or end of a row; Page Up and Page Down go to the top or bottom of a column; Control Home goes to A1.
      • Type a square to jump to it: a letter for the row, then the column number, like C 7 or J 1 4.
      • Enter or Space places a ship or fires.
      • During setup, R rotates between horizontal and vertical. Horizontal ships extend to the right, vertical ships extend down. Choosing a placed ship picks it back up.
      • During battle, S reads fleet status and L repeats the last message.
      • You can also type a square in the Fire at box and press Enter.

      Fleet: aircraft carrier (5), battleship (4), light missile cruiser (3), fast attack sub (3), special operations craft (2).
