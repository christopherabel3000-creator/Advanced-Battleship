          How to play

          The grid has rows A to J from top to bottom and columns 1 to 14 from left to right. A1 is the top left corner and J14 is the bottom right.

          Keys

          • Arrow keys move around a grid. Home and End go to the start or end of a row; Page Up and Page Down go to the top or bottom of a column; Control Home goes to A1.
          • Type a square to jump to it: a letter for the row, then the column number, like C 7 or J 1 4.
          • Enter or Space places a ship or fires. To place a ship, choose one end and then the other, like D4 and then H4; the length picks which ship goes there. Choosing the same square twice places the current ship from there in the Rotate direction. Escape clears a chosen end.
          • In the Place at box, type one square, or both ends of a ship such as D4 H4 or I8 I10.
          • During setup, R rotates between horizontal and vertical. Horizontal ships extend to the right, vertical ships extend down. Choosing a placed ship picks it back up.
          • Against the computer, Restart game (pressed twice) goes back to setup with your fleet where it was.
          • During battle, S reads fleet status and L repeats the last message. You can also type a square in the Fire at box and press Enter.
          • On a phone, use the Place at and Fire at boxes, the Repeat last message and Fleet status buttons, and the Weapon menu instead of keys.
          • Advanced weapons: W picks the next weapon and Shift W the previous one. R switches the search pattern or direction.

          Online play

          Press Host an online game to get a four-letter code and an invite link for your friend. The host's choice of weapons and turn rules applies to both of you. You each place your fleet and press I'm ready, and the host fires first. If you leave or lose your connection, rejoin with the same code and the game picks up where it was.

          Fleet

          Aircraft carrier (5), battleship (4), light missile cruiser (3), fast attack sub (3), special operations craft (2).

          Advanced weapons

          • Attack squadrons: two, placed on your carrier during setup. A squadron flies to any square on the enemy grid and searches in an X pattern (the square and its four diagonal neighbors) or a plus pattern (the square and the four beside it). You learn which searched squares hold a ship. The enemy learns the pattern and which of their ships were seen, but not where the squadron is. The first time a squadron spots an unhit ship square, it must strike one of the spotted squares right away. A squadron still on the carrier is destroyed if that carrier square is hit.
          • Antiaircraft battery: fire at one square of your own grid. It hits only an enemy squadron flying there, which is destroyed. Never runs out.
          • Tomahawk (one, on the battleship): hits the chosen square and all eight around it.
          • Harpoons (two, on the light missile cruiser): hit three squares in a horizontal or vertical line centered on the chosen square.
          • Torpedoes (two, on the fast attack sub): horizontal torpedoes run along the chosen row from column 1; vertical torpedoes run down the chosen column from row A. A torpedo stops at the first ship that isn't sunk, passing over wrecks. Squares it passes are marked clear.
          • Sonar (on the fast attack sub, unlimited): scans the chosen square and the eight around it, and reports enemy ship detected or confirmed clear waters.
          • A ship's weapons are lost when it sinks. Every weapon use takes your turn.

          Target plus 1

          An optional rule for either weapons mode. Any shot or weapon that damages a ship earns another turn, including a squadron's strike and a Tomahawk, harpoon or torpedo that hits anything. An antiaircraft hit on a squadron also earns another turn. Misses, searches that only observe, and sonar end your turn.
